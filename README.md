### 🔭 What I'm currently working on

I'm actively merging my background in Systems Analysis with my ongoing Law studies to build impactful LegalTech and productivity tools. Right now, my focus is on:

* **Jurimetrics & Data Science:** Developing data analysis scripts for my Law monograph, applying jurimetric techniques to explore the intersection of psychopathology and criminal culpability.
* **Productivity & Gamification:** Building **DopamineFocus**, an application designed to help users maintain focus during intensive study or work sessions through gamified rewards and task sorting.
* **Desktop Applications:** Refining **Nexus**, a native Windows ERP system built with React and Electron for offline inventory, sales, and order management.
* **Daily Tech-Law Integration:** Applying automation and tech solutions to streamline administrative and legal workflows in the corporate environment.
